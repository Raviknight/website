# Photo prompts for Gemini

The site works without these photos. Each one replaces a dark placeholder, mostly on the "Who it's built for" tiles on the home page. Send the images back with the file names below, and they'll be cropped, compressed and added.

Rules for every image:
- These are illustrations, not customers' sites. Never caption them as a customer or a real plant.
- No text, logos, brand names or readable screens anywhere in the image.
- Check hands, safety glasses and machine parts for AI mistakes before sending.

Paste the shared style block at the end of each prompt.

**Shared style:** Photorealistic, natural documentary photography, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens, soft natural window light mixed with clean overhead factory lighting, realistic colours with slightly warm tones, shallow depth of field. Landscape 4:3, high resolution. Keep the lower third of the frame simple and darker, because white text will sit over it. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks, no readable computer screens, nobody looking at the camera.

## 1. `tile-machine-shop.jpg`: Machine and job shops

A machinist in a small, tidy CNC job shop checks a freshly machined aluminium part with a digital caliper, standing beside an enclosed CNC milling machine with its door open. The machinist wears safety glasses and a dark work shirt. Background: more CNC machines, a rack of tool holders and a rolling cart of finished parts, slightly out of focus.

## 2. `tile-aerospace.jpg`: Aerospace and defense

A quality inspector in a clean inspection room measures a complex machined aerospace bracket on a granite-table coordinate measuring machine (CMM), with the probe touching the part. The inspector wears safety glasses and a lab coat over work clothes. A printed engineering drawing lies on the table, with its details blurred and unreadable. Cool, precise, calm atmosphere.

## 3. `tile-medical.jpg`: Medical device

A technician in a bright, clean medical device assembly area, wearing a hairnet, gloves and a light gown, inspects a small precision component under a magnifier lamp at a white workbench. Stainless steel surfaces, neatly labelled bins with no readable labels, soft even light. Sterile and orderly.
