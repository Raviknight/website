What Forge covers

Quote to cash

Quotes, orders, work orders, shipping, certificates of conformance and invoicing.

Parts, BOMs and routings

A part master with revisions, bills of materials and routings.

Job costing

See what a job actually cost, and compare it with the quote.

Purchasing and inventory

Purchasing, receiving and stock, with lot traceability.

Quality

Questions we expect

Where does Forge run?

On your own PC or server, using a Windows installer. Your data stays with you.

Does Forge make us compliant?

No software can do that alone. Forge is designed to support your quality system, and your shop is what gets certified.

Do we have to buy everything?

No. Start with Core and add modules when you need them, or take Complete if you need quality, documents, change control and machine scheduling from the start.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $7 per concurrent user per month, and deployment is a one-time charge. See pricing and the calculator.
