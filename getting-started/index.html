Getting started

From your first call to running on your own server.

No long sales cycle and no surprise costs. Here's what happens, and what helps to have ready.

Start with a call See pricing first

Five steps

  1. Tell us how you work

    Use the contact form or email us. Tell us what you use today, roughly how many people and machines, and what's not working.

  2. See it on a job like yours

    We show you the parts that matter to your shop, using a job or machine like yours.

  3. Get a clear quote

    Licence prices are published on the pricing page. Deployment is a one-time charge that depends on your size and the data you want brought in.

  4. Install and bring your data

    We set it up on your own PC or server and import or add the data you want to start with.

  5. Go live

    Start with the part that hurts most, then add more as your team gets comfortable.

What helps to have ready

None of this has to be tidy. Whatever you have in Excel or on paper is a good start.

For PM Tracker

  • Your list of machines and equipment
  • The PMs you do today and how often
  • The checklists or forms your technicians fill in
  • Who does the work and who approves it

For Forge

  • Your customers, suppliers and parts
  • Bills of materials and routings, where you have them
  • Open orders and jobs
  • The quality forms you use today, such as NCRs

Questions we expect

What do we need to run it?

A PC or server on your own network. Forge installs with a Windows installer. We'll go through the details with you before you buy.

How long does deployment take?

It depends on your size and how much data you want brought in. We'll tell you when we quote.

Do we have to move everything at once?

No. Many shops start with one area, such as maintenance or scheduling, and add the rest later.

What happens to our data if we stop?

It stays on your server. When a PM Tracker licence ends, the system goes read-only after the grace period, so you can still see everything.
